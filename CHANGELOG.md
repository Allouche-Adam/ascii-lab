# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-17
- After any download (PNG, SVG or video), a thank-you popup suggests buying a coffee. The download has already started; "maybe later", Escape or a click outside closes it.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-17
- First public version: pictures, GIFs (Chrome/Edge), videos and webcam to colored ASCII.
- Character sets (classic, fine, blocks, dots, katakana, binary, custom), real colors / tint / gradient, background, glow, invert.
- Effects: still, shimmer, glitch, decode, rain, breathe. Save as PNG, animated SVG, WebM video.
- Fix found while testing: some WebM files don't store their length, which showed "Infinity s"; they now show "video" and record 6 s.
